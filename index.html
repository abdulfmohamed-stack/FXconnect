Customer messages to try

Tap one to send it as the customer, or type your own. Muse understands amounts, CAD→USD or USD→CAD, a location, and follow-ups like "lock option 2".

Rules shown in the demo: locks run C$100 to C$9,000, last until the exchange closes, and need government ID at pickup. No payment is taken in WhatsApp.

Demo only. Exchange names, rates, hours and cash levels are invented. A live version needs Muse available in Canada with FXConnect approved as a connector, and each exchange publishing rates to FXConnect. FXConnect never holds customer funds; the exchange completes the transaction at its counter.
